# Requirements Document

## Introduction

Swedish church books — above all the Husförhörslängd (series AI/AII) — do not record events. A volume covering 1866–1870 proves that a person was resident at a place during *at least* that span. It does not prove that the person moved in when the volume starts, nor that the person moved out when it ends. The existing `Event` entity carries a single `DateValue` and therefore cannot express a state that holds over an interval with uncertainty at each end.

This feature introduces a new first-class entity, the Residence_Fact ("Boende"): one fact per person, per place, per interval, where each interval endpoint is a *range* rather than a point. Evidence is attached as Observations — one per church book volume/page — each keeping its own sub-interval, so it stays visible which volume proves which years. Gaps between observation sub-intervals surface as research suggestions.

There is deliberately no household entity. "Who lived at Farm X in 1875" is answered as a query over residence facts whose interval contains 1875. This handles the common Swedish pattern where parents remain on a farm for decades while children are born, move away, move back, have their own children, and move away again.

The entity is source-neutral: Husförhörslängd, Församlingsbok, Mantalslängd, and Folkräkning all attach as Observations, where a single-year source is simply an Observation whose `observed_from` and `observed_to` are equal.

The workflow runs period first. The user records the period as their own conclusion, with no sources attached yet, and then attaches volumes to it one at a time — commonly ten to fifteen for one continuous residence. Each volume attests only the part of the period it actually covers for that person, so an Observation's span may be narrower than the volume's coverage. As further volumes turn up, the period is extended and the new sources attached under it. Observations therefore only ever tighten what is documented inside the period; the period's outer bounds stay the user's own.

The six previously open decisions are settled; the answers are recorded under "Settled Decisions" at the end of this document.

## Glossary

- **Residence_Fact**: The new entity, displayed in Swedish as "Boende" (plural "Boenden") with the internal key `residence`. States that one person was resident at one place over one interval. Holds `id`, `person_id`, `place_id`, `start`, `end`, `role_in_household`, `observations`, `notes`.
- **Endpoint**: The boundary of a Residence_Fact interval, expressed as a range with `earliest`, `latest`, `precision`, optional `event_id`, and `note`. Either bound may be absent, which makes the endpoint open.
- **Open_Endpoint**: An Endpoint where the bound facing away from the interval is absent — a start with no `earliest`, or an end with no `latest`. Expresses "extends an unknown amount in that direction".
- **Certain_Core**: The sub-interval of a Residence_Fact during which residence is documented rather than possible: from `start.latest` to `end.earliest`, empty when either bound is absent or when `start.latest` falls after `end.earliest`. Its two bounds are the only bounds that Observations may move, and they may move only in the tightening direction — see Documented core tightening.
- **Possible_Span**: The widest interval a Residence_Fact could cover: from `start.earliest` to `end.latest`, unbounded in a direction whose bound is absent.
- **Observation**: A single evidence entry on a Residence_Fact. Holds a `SourceRef` to a page-level Source plus `observed_from`, `observed_to`, and `page_note`. Its `observed_from` and `observed_to` record the period the Source attests for that person, which may be narrower than the coverage period of the volume the Source belongs to — a volume covering 1847–1853 for a person who moved in during 1851 attests only 1851–1853. The volume's coverage period stays on the Source as its `structured_reference` `years` value.
- **Documented core tightening**: The rule that attaching, editing, or removing an Observation may only move `start.latest` earlier and `end.earliest` later, and may never modify the outer bounds `start.earliest` and `end.latest`. The period is the user's own conclusion; Observations only ever narrow what is documented within it. Covered by Requirement 16.
- **Flytt_Event**: An Event of the new individual type `flytt`, displayed in Swedish as "Flytt". A point in time, not an interval, so it lives in the existing `Event` entity rather than in a Residence_Fact. It carries the destination in the existing `place` field and the origin in a new optional `from_place` field, either of which may be absent to mean unknown. One Flytt_Event may be linked from the end Endpoint of one Residence_Fact and the start Endpoint of another, fixing both as exact dates. Covered by Requirement 18.
- **Residence_Model**: The dataclasses and pure functions defining Residence_Fact, Endpoint, and Observation.
- **Residence_Validator**: The validation function for Residence_Fact, following the conventions of `slaktbusken/model/validators.py`.
- **Coverage_Analyzer**: The service computing observation coverage, coverage gaps, open endpoints, and person-timeline gaps, and turning them into research suggestions.
- **Residence_Inference**: The service computing tightened endpoint bounds from neighbouring Residence_Facts and from birth/death events, without mutating stored data.
- **Residence_Query_Service**: The service answering "who was resident at place P during year Y" and "where was person X during year Y".
- **Residence_Formatter**: The formatting functions in `slaktbusken/ui/swedish_locale.py` rendering intervals, endpoints, and observation spans as Swedish display strings.
- **Residence_Editor**: The PySide6 panel for creating and editing Residence_Facts, including bulk entry from pasted references.
- **Reference_Parser**: The existing `slaktbusken/parsing/reference_parser.py` module (`parse_reference`, `parse_multi_line`).
- **Delete_Service**: The existing `slaktbusken/services/delete_service.py` module, which performs cascading deletion of dependent entities and reports blocking dependencies that prevent a deletion from proceeding.
- **Person_Check_Engine**: The existing `slaktbusken/services/person_check_engine.py` module, which runs per-person checks and presents their findings as research to-dos.
- **Serialization module**: The existing `slaktbusken/persistence/serialization.py` module, which writes the Project to the project file and reads it back.
- **Migration_Manager**: The existing `slaktbusken/persistence/migration.py` module, which registers and applies project file format migrations and guards the stored format version.
- **GEDCOM_Exporter**: The existing `slaktbusken/gedcom/exporter.py` module, which writes GEDCOM 5.5.1 output.
- **GEDCOM_Importer**: The existing `slaktbusken/gedcom/importer.py` module, which reads GEDCOM input.
- **Source_Aspects**: The existing `slaktbusken/services/source_aspects.py` module, which defines per-entity aspect lists that a `SourceRef` may cite.
- **Project**: The root container `ProjectData` holding all entities.
- **Source**: An existing page-level source record. For `source_type` "church_book" its `structured_reference` carries `parish`, `series`, `volume`, `years`, `image`, `page`.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Residence Fact Entity

**User Story:** As a genealogist, I want to record that a person lived at a place over a period, so that I can register what a Husförhörslängd actually proves instead of inventing move events.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Residence_Model SHALL define a Residence_Fact whose fields `id`, `person_id`, `place_id`, `start`, and `end` are required, where `start` and `end` are each an Endpoint, and whose fields `role_in_household` as text, `observations` as an ordered list, and `notes` as text each default to empty.
2. THE Project SHALL hold Residence_Facts in a `residences` collection alongside `events`, SHALL initialize that collection as empty for a newly created Project, and SHALL preserve the order in which Residence_Facts were added.
3. THE Residence_Model SHALL accept a `place_id` referencing a place of any type, including "farm", "ort", and any region level such as socken, and THE Residence_Validator SHALL return zero errors based on the referenced place's type.
4. THE Residence_Model SHALL accept two or more Residence_Facts having the same `person_id` and `place_id` combination, including facts whose intervals are identical, and THE Residence_Validator SHALL return zero errors for that combination.
5. IF `person_id` holds a non-empty value that is absent from the Project persons collection, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return exactly one occurrence of the error message "Boendet refererar till en person som inte finns."
6. IF `place_id` holds a non-empty value that is absent from the Project places collection, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return exactly one occurrence of the error message "Boendet refererar till en plats som inte finns."
7. IF `person_id` or `place_id` is empty or contains only whitespace, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return exactly one occurrence of the error message "Boendet måste ange både person och plats." and SHALL return no missing-reference message for that same field.
8. WHEN a person is deleted from the Project, THE Delete_Service SHALL delete every Residence_Fact whose `person_id` equals that person's identifier together with the Observations those Residence_Facts hold, and SHALL leave every other Residence_Fact unchanged.
9. IF deletion of a place is requested while one or more Residence_Facts reference that place, THEN THE Delete_Service SHALL refuse the deletion, SHALL report one blocking dependency entry per referencing Residence_Fact, and SHALL leave the place and the `residences` collection unchanged.
10. THE Residence_Model SHALL accept `notes` holding 0 to 5000 characters.
11. IF `notes` exceeds 5000 characters, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return the error message "Anteckningen får vara högst 5000 tecken."
12. WHEN a Residence_Fact is added to the Project, THE Project SHALL assign it an `id` that is unique among all identifiers in the Project and SHALL never reuse that identifier after the Residence_Fact is deleted.
13. WHEN THE Residence_Validator checks a Residence_Fact that violates none of the stated rules, THE Residence_Validator SHALL return an empty list of Swedish error messages.

### Requirement 2: Endpoint Range Semantics

**User Story:** As a genealogist, I want each end of a residence period to be a range rather than a date, so that "present by 1840, arrived at an unknown earlier time" is recorded as exactly that.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Endpoint SHALL define the fields `earliest`, `latest`, `precision`, `event_id`, and `note`, where `earliest`, `latest`, and `event_id` are each optional, where `note` holds at most 1000 characters and defaults to empty, and where a value that is empty or contains only whitespace counts as absent throughout this requirement.
2. THE Endpoint SHALL accept `earliest` and `latest` values matching the existing ISO 8601 forms ÅÅÅÅ, ÅÅÅÅ-MM, and ÅÅÅÅ-MM-DD.
3. THE Endpoint SHALL accept `precision` values from the existing set day, month, year, approximate, and THE Residence_Model SHALL treat `precision` as descriptive only, so that its value never alters Endpoint classification, validation, or bound derivation.
4. WHERE `earliest` and `latest` are both present and denote the same day interval, THE Residence_Model SHALL classify the Endpoint as an exact date.
5. WHERE `earliest` is absent and `latest` is present, THE Residence_Model SHALL classify the Endpoint as open and interpret it as "the person was present at the place by the last day of `latest`, and the transition occurred at an unknown earlier time".
6. WHERE `earliest` is present and `latest` is absent, THE Residence_Model SHALL classify the Endpoint as open and interpret it as "the person was present at the place on the first day of `earliest`, and the transition occurred at an unknown later time".
7. WHERE `earliest` and `latest` are both present and denote different day intervals, THE Residence_Model SHALL classify the Endpoint as a transition window spanning the first day of `earliest` through the last day of `latest` and interpret it as "the transition occurred at an unknown point inside the window".
8. WHERE `earliest` and `latest` are both absent, THE Residence_Model SHALL classify the Endpoint as unknown and THE Residence_Validator SHALL return zero errors and zero warning-level findings for that Endpoint.
9. IF `earliest` and `latest` are both present and the last day of `latest` precedes the first day of `earliest`, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return the error message "Tidigaste datum får inte vara senare än senaste datum.", so that an `earliest` of "1840" together with a `latest` of "1840-06" is no error.
10. IF `start.earliest` and `end.latest` are both present and the last day of `end.latest` precedes the first day of `start.earliest`, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return the error message "Boendets slut kan inte ligga före dess början." and SHALL return no error for any other relationship between the bounds of the two Endpoints, so that an impossible Possible_Span is the only hard error defined across the two Endpoints and an overlap of the start and end windows is reported as a warning under criterion 17.
11. IF an `earliest` or `latest` value is present and fails the ISO 8601 form check, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return exactly one occurrence of the error message "Datumvärdet är inte ett giltigt ISO 8601-datum (förväntat ÅÅÅÅ, ÅÅÅÅ-MM eller ÅÅÅÅ-MM-DD)." per offending value and SHALL leave that value unchanged.
12. WHERE `event_id` is present, IF the identifier references no Event in the Project events collection, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return the error message "Endpunkten refererar till en händelse som inte finns."
13. THE Residence_Model SHALL derive the Certain_Core of a Residence_Fact as the interval from the last day of `start.latest` through the first day of `end.earliest`, SHALL derive it as empty when either bound is absent or when the last day of `start.latest` falls after the first day of `end.earliest`, and SHALL perform that derivation as a pure function that returns the derived interval and leaves the Residence_Fact unchanged.
14. IF an Endpoint `note` exceeds 1000 characters, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return the error message "Endpunktens anteckning får vara högst 1000 tecken."
15. THE Residence_Model SHALL expand each present `earliest` and `latest` value to the closed day interval that value denotes, where ÅÅÅÅ spans 1 January through 31 December of that year, ÅÅÅÅ-MM spans the first through the last day of that month, and ÅÅÅÅ-MM-DD spans that single day, and SHALL treat value A as earlier than value B only when the last day of A precedes the first day of B, so that "1840" is neither earlier nor later than "1840-06".
16. THE Residence_Model SHALL derive the Possible_Span of a Residence_Fact as the interval from the first day of `start.earliest` through the last day of `end.latest`, unbounded in a direction whose bound is absent, and SHALL perform that derivation as a pure function that returns the derived interval and leaves the Residence_Fact unchanged.
17. WHERE `start.latest` and `end.earliest` are both present and the last day of `start.latest` falls after the first day of `end.earliest`, THE Residence_Validator SHALL return exactly one warning-level finding with the message "Boendets start- och slutfönster överlappar – ingen dokumenterad kärnperiod." and zero errors.

### Requirement 3: Linking an Endpoint to an Event

**User Story:** As a genealogist, I want to state that a residence ended because the person died, so that the endpoint is anchored to the event that fixes it instead of repeating the date by hand.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Residence_Editor SHALL offer, for each Endpoint, a selector whose first item is an empty choice meaning "no linked event", followed by every Event whose `participants` include the Residence_Fact's `person_id`, ordered by Event date ascending with undated Events last, and WHERE the person participates in zero Events THE Residence_Editor SHALL present the selector holding only the empty choice and accepting no selection.
2. THE Residence_Editor SHALL label each Event in the selector with its Swedish event type label followed by the formatted Event date in parentheses, and SHALL label an Event whose date is absent with its Swedish event type label alone.
3. WHEN the user selects an Event whose date is present in an Endpoint selector, including re-selecting the Event already linked, THE Residence_Editor SHALL set that Endpoint's `event_id` to the selected Event's identifier, `earliest` and `latest` to the Event date value, and `precision` to the Event date precision.
4. WHEN the user selects an Event whose date is absent in an Endpoint selector, THE Residence_Editor SHALL set that Endpoint's `event_id` to the selected Event's identifier and SHALL keep its `earliest`, `latest`, and `precision` values unchanged.
5. WHILE an Endpoint has `event_id` present and referencing an existing Event, THE Residence_Editor SHALL display next to that Endpoint's fields the same label form that criterion 2 defines for the selector.
6. WHEN the user selects the empty choice in an Endpoint selector, THE Residence_Editor SHALL set that Endpoint's `event_id` to absent and SHALL keep the `earliest`, `latest`, and `precision` values that were displayed immediately before the selection.
7. WHILE an Endpoint has `event_id` present and referencing an existing Event whose date value differs from that Endpoint's `earliest` or from its `latest`, THE Residence_Editor SHALL display the message "Kopplad händelse har ett annat datum än endpunkten – uppdatera endpunkten." and SHALL keep the stored Endpoint values unchanged, re-evaluating the comparison each time the Residence_Fact is loaded into the editor and each time an Endpoint field or Endpoint Event link is changed, and SHALL display no such message when only `precision` differs.
8. WHILE an Endpoint has `event_id` present, THE Residence_Editor SHALL keep that Endpoint's `earliest`, `latest`, and `precision` fields editable and SHALL leave `event_id` unchanged when the user enters new values into them.
9. IF the user selects a death Event for a start Endpoint or a birth Event for an end Endpoint, THEN THE Residence_Editor SHALL apply the link as defined in criteria 3 and 4 and SHALL display the message "Vald händelse hör normalt till boendets andra endpunkt – kontrollera kopplingen.", and WHERE the selected Event is a Flytt_Event, THE Residence_Editor SHALL display no such message for either endpoint, a Flytt_Event being valid on a start Endpoint and on an end Endpoint alike as defined in Requirement 18.
10. IF an Endpoint `event_id` references an identifier absent from the Project events collection, THEN THE Residence_Editor SHALL display the message "Händelsen saknas – rensa kopplingen." for that Endpoint, SHALL offer the empty choice as defined in criterion 6, and SHALL keep the Endpoint `earliest`, `latest`, and `precision` values unchanged until the user changes them.
11. WHEN an Event is deleted from the Project, THE Delete_Service SHALL set `event_id` to absent on every Endpoint of every Residence_Fact referencing that Event, SHALL keep those Endpoints' `earliest`, `latest`, and `precision` values unchanged, and SHALL delete no Residence_Fact.

### Requirement 4: Observations as the Evidence Layer

**User Story:** As a genealogist with ten consecutive HFL volumes for the same person at the same farm, I want all ten attached to one residence fact while keeping each volume's own years, so that it stays visible which volume proves which period.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Observation SHALL define the fields `source_ref`, `observed_from`, `observed_to`, and `page_note`, where `source_ref` is an existing `SourceRef` carrying `source_id`, `quality`, `note`, and `aspects`, where `observed_from` and `observed_to` each hold either an empty value or a four-digit year in the form ÅÅÅÅ within the range 1500 to 2100, and where `page_note` holds at most 1000 characters, and THE Residence_Model SHALL treat `observed_from` and `observed_to` as the period the referenced Source attests for this person, which may be narrower than the coverage period of the volume the Source belongs to, the volume's own coverage period remaining available on the Source as its `structured_reference` `years` value and never being overwritten by the Observation.
2. THE Residence_Model SHALL accept zero to 100 Observations on a single Residence_Fact and SHALL preserve each Observation's own `observed_from` and `observed_to` values independently of every other Observation, and SHALL otherwise return the error message "Ett boende får ha högst 100 observationer."
3. THE Residence_Model SHALL append each newly added Observation after the Observations already present and SHALL return the Observations of a Residence_Fact in that order, keeping the relative order of the remaining Observations unchanged when one is removed.
4. IF `observed_from` and `observed_to` are both present and `observed_from` is later than `observed_to`, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return the error message "Observationens startår får inte vara senare än dess slutår."
5. IF an Observation's `source_ref.source_id` references an identifier absent from the Project sources collection, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return the error message "Observationen refererar till en källa som inte finns."
6. WHERE exactly one of an Observation's `observed_from` and `observed_to` holds a year, THE Residence_Model SHALL treat that Observation as covering that single year, and THE Residence_Validator SHALL return zero errors for that Observation.
7. WHEN the user attaches a Source of type "church_book" to an Observation whose `observed_from` and `observed_to` are both empty, and the Source `structured_reference` `years` field holds two four-digit years within the range 1500 to 2100 in ascending order separated by a hyphen or an en dash with any number of surrounding spaces, THE Residence_Editor SHALL prefill `observed_from` with the first year and `observed_to` with the second year as an editable starting suggestion derived from the volume's coverage period, and SHALL treat that suggestion as no assertion about the person's presence.
8. WHEN the user attaches a Source to an Observation whose `observed_from` and `observed_to` are both empty, and the Source `years` field holds a single four-digit year within the range 1500 to 2100, THE Residence_Editor SHALL prefill both `observed_from` and `observed_to` with that year as an editable starting suggestion derived from the volume's coverage period, and SHALL treat that suggestion as no assertion about the person's presence.
9. IF the `years` field is absent, empty, holds a value that is neither a single four-digit year nor two four-digit years in ascending order separated by a hyphen or an en dash, or holds a year outside the range 1500 to 2100, THEN THE Residence_Editor SHALL skip prefilling entirely, leaving `observed_from` and `observed_to` empty, and SHALL display the message "Kunde inte läsa årtal från källan – ange period manuellt."
10. WHEN the user types a year into `observed_from` or `observed_to`, THE Residence_Editor SHALL store the typed value in place of any prefilled value, SHALL apply no further prefill to that Observation, SHALL never later replace or re-suggest that value from the Source `years` value, and WHERE an Observation's span is narrower than the `years` value of its Source, THE Residence_Editor and THE Residence_Validator SHALL return zero warnings and zero findings for that difference, a narrower span being the expected state when the person was present for only part of the volume's coverage period.
11. THE Residence_Model SHALL accept a Residence_Fact carrying Observations while both of its Endpoints are unknown, so that an undated but sourced residence fact has a place to hold its citations.
12. IF an Observation's `observed_from` or `observed_to` is non-empty and is not a four-digit year in the form ÅÅÅÅ within the range 1500 to 2100, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return the error message "Observationens årtal måste anges som fyra siffror (ÅÅÅÅ) mellan 1500 och 2100."
13. IF two or more Observations on the same Residence_Fact carry the same `source_ref.source_id`, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return a warning-level finding with the message "Samma källa är kopplad till boendet mer än en gång." and THE Residence_Editor SHALL save the Residence_Fact with its Observations unchanged.
14. THE Source_Aspects module SHALL define a residence aspect list containing "place", "period", "household_role", and "household_members", with the Swedish labels "Plats", "Period", "Hushållsroll", and "Hushållsmedlemmar", and SHALL return that list for the internal key `residence`.

### Requirement 5: Coverage Gaps and Research Suggestions

**User Story:** As a genealogist, I want the app to point out that my volumes cover 1866–1870, 1871–1875 and 1881–1885 but not 1876–1880, so that I know which volume to look up next.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL compute the coverage union of a Residence_Fact as the set of whole calendar years contributed by its Observations, where each Observation contributes every year from the year part of `observed_from` to the year part of `observed_to` inclusive, and where a year contributed by two or more identical, overlapping, or adjacent Observations appears exactly once in the union.
2. WHEN a Residence_Fact carries two or more Observations and at least one whole year between the lowest year part of `observed_from` and the highest year part of `observed_to` among those Observations is absent from the coverage union, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL report one coverage gap per maximal run of consecutive absent years, each gap stating its first and last uncovered year, ordered by first uncovered year ascending.
3. WHEN a Residence_Fact carries zero Observations, carries exactly one Observation, or has a coverage union containing every year between the lowest year part of `observed_from` and the highest year part of `observed_to`, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL report zero coverage gaps for that Residence_Fact.
4. WHEN THE Coverage_Analyzer reports a coverage gap, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL leave every field of the Residence_Fact, of its Observations, and of every other Project entity unchanged.
5. WHERE a reported coverage gap spans two or more uncovered years, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL phrase the research suggestion in the form "Ljusdal AI: period 1876–1880 saknar källa", taking the parish and series values from the `church_book` fields of the Source referenced by the Observation whose covered years end immediately before the gap, and omitting an absent parish or series value together with its separating space.
6. WHERE the Project holds one or more Sources whose `church_book` parish and series equal those used in the suggestion and whose `years` value contains at least one uncovered year of a reported gap, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL name at most the five such Sources with the lowest first year, ordered by first year ascending, appending their series and volume values to the suggestion in the form "Ljusdal AI: period 1876–1880 saknar källa – kontrollera AI:18".
7. WHERE a Residence_Fact has an Open_Endpoint, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL report one research suggestion per open bound, naming "början" for a start Endpoint whose `earliest` is absent and "slutet" for an end Endpoint whose `latest` is absent, and SHALL report at most two such suggestions for one Residence_Fact.
8. WHEN a person has two or more Residence_Facts and at least one whole year between the lowest and the highest bounded year of their Possible_Spans is contained in no Possible_Span of that person's Residence_Facts, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL report one timeline gap per maximal run of consecutive such years, each stating its first and last uncovered year, and SHALL treat a Possible_Span with an absent outward bound as containing every year in that direction.
9. THE Person_Check_Engine SHALL present coverage gaps, open endpoints, and timeline gaps as findings on the person, ordered by first uncovered year ascending with open endpoint findings last, completing a check run over a Project holding up to 10 000 Residence_Facts in at most 5 seconds.
10. WHEN THE Coverage_Analyzer reports a coverage gap for which at least one Observation of that Residence_Fact ends before the gap's first uncovered year and at least one Observation begins after the gap's last uncovered year, THE Residence_Editor SHALL offer the action "Dela boendet här" for that gap.
11. WHEN the user invokes "Dela boendet här" for a reported coverage gap having at least one Observation on each side of the gap, THE Residence_Editor SHALL replace the Residence_Fact with exactly two Residence_Facts, assigning every Observation whose year part of `observed_to` is earlier than the gap's first uncovered year to the first Residence_Fact and every Observation whose year part of `observed_from` is later than the gap's last uncovered year to the second Residence_Fact.
12. WHEN THE Residence_Editor splits a Residence_Fact, THE Residence_Editor SHALL give each resulting Residence_Fact a new unique `id`, SHALL copy `person_id`, `place_id`, `role_in_household`, and `notes` unchanged to both resulting Residence_Facts, SHALL keep the original `start` Endpoint on the first Residence_Fact and the original `end` Endpoint on the second Residence_Fact, SHALL set the first Residence_Fact `end.earliest` to the highest `observed_to` among the Observations assigned to it and the second Residence_Fact `start.latest` to the lowest `observed_from` among the Observations assigned to it, and SHALL leave the first Residence_Fact `end.latest` and the second Residence_Fact `start.earliest` absent.
13. WHEN THE Residence_Editor splits a Residence_Fact, THE Residence_Editor SHALL produce a combined Observation set across the two resulting Residence_Facts equal to the original Observation set, with each Observation appearing exactly once, keeping its `source_ref`, `observed_from`, `observed_to`, and `page_note` unchanged, and keeping the original relative order within each resulting Residence_Fact.
14. WHERE a reported coverage gap spans exactly one uncovered year, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL phrase the research suggestion in the form "Ljusdal AI: år 1876 saknar källa".
15. WHERE the person has a birth Event with a date, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL exclude every year earlier than the year part of that birth date from timeline gap reporting, and WHERE the person has a death Event with a date, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL exclude every year later than the year part of that death date from timeline gap reporting.
16. IF the user invokes "Dela boendet här" for a gap that no longer has at least one Observation ending before its first uncovered year and at least one Observation beginning after its last uncovered year, THEN THE Residence_Editor SHALL leave the Residence_Fact and its Observations unchanged and SHALL display an error message indicating that the boende cannot be split because observations exist on only one side of the period.

### Requirement 6: Overlapping Residence Facts

**User Story:** As a genealogist, I want the app to allow the messy transition years where two sources place a person at two farms, while still flagging the overlap for me to look at.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Residence_Model SHALL store two Residence_Facts for the same person with overlapping Possible_Spans and THE Residence_Validator SHALL return zero errors for that pair, whether the two `place_id` values are equal or different, warning-level findings still being returned as defined in criteria 4 and 5.
2. THE Residence_Validator SHALL treat the Certain_Cores of two Residence_Facts as overlapping only when the start of the later Certain_Core is strictly earlier than the end of the earlier Certain_Core, compared at the coarser of the two Certain_Core precisions, so that two Certain_Cores sharing exactly one boundary value are touching rather than overlapping and produce zero overlap findings, as in the Requirement 14 case of Place A `end.earliest` 1840 together with Place B start 1840.
3. THE Residence_Validator SHALL evaluate overlap only for pairs of Residence_Facts whose `person_id` values are equal, SHALL return zero overlap findings for a pair whose `person_id` values differ, and SHALL pair no Residence_Fact with itself.
4. WHEN two Residence_Facts for the same person at different places have overlapping Certain_Cores as defined in criterion 2, THE Residence_Validator SHALL return a warning-level finding with the message "Överlappande boenden: {plats A} och {plats B} överlappar {period}.", where {period} is the intersection of the two Certain_Cores rendered as a closed interval by THE Residence_Formatter.

### Requirement 7: Inferred Tightening from Neighbours

**User Story:** As a genealogist, I want the app to show me what neighbouring facts imply about an open endpoint, without silently rewriting what my sources actually say.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. 2. WHEN THE Residence_Inference computes derived bounds, THE Residence_Inference SHALL leave every stored field of every Residence_Fact, Endpoint, and Observation unchanged, and SHALL exclude the derived bounds from the data written to the project file.
3. WHERE a Residence_Fact start Endpoint has no `earliest`, THE Residence_Inference SHALL form one neighbour candidate for that start `earliest` as the latest stored `end.earliest` among the Residence_Facts of the same person whose `place_id` differs from that of the fact and whose stored `end.earliest` is earlier than the fact's `start.latest`, SHALL use the fact's stored `end.earliest` as the comparison reference when the fact's `start.latest` is absent, and SHALL form no neighbour candidate when the fact's `start.latest` and `end.earliest` are both absent or when no neighbouring value satisfies the comparison.
4. WHERE a Residence_Fact start Endpoint has no `earliest` and the person has at least one birth Event with a date, THE Residence_Inference SHALL form one birth candidate for that start `earliest` as the earliest such birth date, for every Residence_Fact of that person regardless of the fact's position in the person's timeline.
5. 6. WHERE two or more candidates exist for the same bound of the same Endpoint, THE Residence_Inference SHALL derive an `earliest` bound as the latest candidate and a `latest` bound as the earliest candidate, SHALL compare candidates of differing precision by expanding each value to its widest interval, so that a ÅÅÅÅ or ÅÅÅÅ-MM value competes as its first day when compared as an `earliest` candidate and as its last day when compared as a `latest` candidate, and SHALL return the winning candidate in the ISO 8601 form in which it was stored.

WHEN THE Residence_Editor displays a bound whose stored value is absent and for which THE Residence_Inference returned a derived value, THE Residence_Editor SHALL display the derived value followed by the Swedish label "härlett" so that it reads differently from a stored bound, SHALL present that value as read-only, and SHALL keep the stored bound absent when the Residence_Fact is saved without the action "Snäva in från grannar" being invoked.
8. WHEN the user invokes the action "Snäva in från grannar" for a selected Residence_Fact and THE Residence_Inference returns one or more derived bounds, THE Residence_Editor SHALL present a confirmation dialog listing one row per derived bound showing the endpoint (start or end), the bound name, the stored value or "okänt" when the stored bound is absent, the proposed value, and the neighbouring place or the Event the value was derived from, SHALL preselect every listed row, SHALL allow the user to deselect any subset of rows individually, and SHALL leave every stored field unchanged until the user confirms the dialog.
9. WHEN the user confirms the "Snäva in från grannar" dialog with one or more rows selected, THE Residence_Editor SHALL write only the selected proposed values into the corresponding stored Endpoint bounds of that Residence_Fact, SHALL leave every deselected bound absent, and SHALL leave `precision`, `event_id`, `note`, and every Observation of that Residence_Fact unchanged.
10. WHEN the user invokes "Snäva in från grannar" a second time on the same Residence_Fact, with no intervening change to any Residence_Fact, Event, or Endpoint of that person and with the same rows selected, THE Residence_Editor SHALL leave every stored Endpoint bound of that Residence_Fact equal to its value after the first invocation, because THE Residence_Inference forms no candidate for a bound that is already present.
11. IF THE Residence_Inference returns zero derived bounds for the Residence_Fact selected when the user invokes "Snäva in från grannar", THEN THE Residence_Editor SHALL display the message "Inga härledda värden att föreslå.", SHALL present no confirmation dialog, and SHALL leave every stored field unchanged.
12. IF a candidate for a start `earliest` is later than the stored `start.latest` or later than the stored `end.latest` of the same Residence_Fact, or a candidate for an end `latest` is earlier than the stored `end.earliest` or earlier than the stored `start.earliest` of the same Residence_Fact, THEN THE Residence_Inference SHALL omit that candidate from the derived bounds, SHALL report a warning-level finding with the message "Härlett värde motsäger inmatat värde.", and SHALL return the remaining derived bounds unchanged.

### Requirement 14: Worked Example — Fully Accounted Sequence

**User Story:** As a genealogist, I want to record that Anders was at Place A until 1840, at Place B from 1840 until his death in 1846, so that his adult life has no unexplained gaps.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Residence_Model SHALL accept a Residence_Fact at Place A with a start Endpoint holding `latest` 1837 and no `earliest`, and an end Endpoint holding `earliest` 1840 and `latest` 1840, returning zero errors, and SHALL derive its Certain_Core as 1837 through 1840 and its Possible_Span as unbounded earlier through 1840.
2. THE Residence_Model SHALL accept a Residence_Fact at Place B with a start Endpoint holding `earliest` 1840 and `latest` 1840, and an end Endpoint holding `earliest` 1846, `latest` 1846, and an `event_id` referencing the person's death Event, returning zero errors, and SHALL derive both its Certain_Core and its Possible_Span as 1840 through 1846.
3. WHEN THE Coverage_Analyzer analyses the two Residence_Facts together, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL report zero timeline gaps between them, the Place A `end.earliest` 1840 and the Place B start 1840 sharing the boundary year 1840, which counts as touching rather than as overlapping.
4. WHEN THE Residence_Validator checks the two Residence_Facts together, THE Residence_Validator SHALL report zero overlap findings, the shared boundary year 1840 counting as touching rather than as overlapping under Requirement 6 criterion 2.
5. WHEN THE Coverage_Analyzer analyses the two Residence_Facts, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL report exactly one open-endpoint research suggestion, naming the Place A start Endpoint, and zero research suggestions for the Place A end Endpoint, the Place B start Endpoint, and the Place B end Endpoint.
6. THE Residence_Formatter SHALL render the Place A interval as "senast 1837–1840".
7. THE Residence_Formatter SHALL render the Place B interval as "1840–1846".
8. WHEN THE Residence_Query_Service is asked for the residents of Place B in 1850, THE Residence_Query_Service SHALL return zero persons, the Place B end being fixed at 1846 by the death Event.
9. WHEN THE Residence_Query_Service is asked for the residence timeline of the person, THE Residence_Query_Service SHALL return exactly two Residence_Facts with the Place A Residence_Fact first, its absent `start.earliest` sorting earliest under Requirement 8 criterion 7.

### Requirement 15: Worked Example — Both Endpoints Open

**User Story:** As a genealogist, I want to record that Brita was at Place C at least 1840–1846 and probably longer, so that the uncertainty stays visible as a research to-do.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Residence_Model SHALL accept a Residence_Fact at Place C with a start Endpoint holding `latest` 1840 and no `earliest`, and an end Endpoint holding `earliest` 1846 and no `latest`, returning zero errors and zero warning-level findings, and SHALL classify both Endpoints as open under Requirement 2.
2. WHEN THE Coverage_Analyzer analyses that Residence_Fact, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL report exactly two open-endpoint research suggestions, one for the start Endpoint and one for the end Endpoint, and SHALL leave the Residence_Fact unchanged.
3. THE Residence_Formatter SHALL render that interval as "senast 1840–tidigast 1846".
4. THE Residence_Formatter SHALL render the Requirement 14 Place B interval as "1840–1846" and the Place C interval as "senast 1840–tidigast 1846", which are two strings that are not equal.
5. WHEN THE Residence_Query_Service is asked for the residents of Place C in 1850, THE Residence_Query_Service SHALL return exactly one result labelled "möjlig", stating as the reason that 1850 lies inside the Possible_Span and outside the Certain_Core.
6. WHEN THE Residence_Query_Service is asked for the residents of Place C in 1840, in 1843, or in 1846, THE Residence_Query_Service SHALL return the person with the label "säker", both Certain_Core bounds counting as inside the Certain_Core.
7. THE Residence_Model SHALL derive the Place C Certain_Core as 1840 through 1846 inclusive and the Place C Possible_Span as unbounded in both directions, the Certain_Core being non-empty even though both Endpoints are open.
8. WHERE the Observations of the Place C Residence_Fact cover every year from 1840 to 1846 with no uncovered year, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL report zero coverage gaps for that Residence_Fact, independently of the two open-endpoint research suggestions of criterion 2.
9. WHEN THE Residence_Query_Service is asked for the residents of Place C in 1750 or in 1980, THE Residence_Query_Service SHALL return the person with the label "möjlig", applying no clamping to the person's lifespan, the narrowing of an unbounded Possible_Span occurring only through the user-invoked action of Requirement 7, consistent with Requirement 8 querying stored Possible_Spans and Requirement 7 leaving stored data unchanged.

### Requirement 16: Period-First Lifecycle and Attaching Sources

**User Story:** As a genealogist, I want to record the period first and then attach ten to fifteen volumes to it one at a time, so that the period stays my own conclusion while the sources accumulate underneath it.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the user creates a Residence_Fact holding a `person_id`, a `place_id`, hand-entered Endpoint bounds, and zero Observations, THE Residence_Validator SHALL return zero errors, so that a period may exist before any Source is attached to it.
2. THE Residence_Editor SHALL present `start.earliest`, `start.latest`, `end.earliest`, and `end.latest` as four directly editable fields with the Swedish labels "Tidigast början", "Senast början", "Tidigast slut", and "Senast slut", SHALL accept in each field the ISO 8601 forms defined in Requirement 2 criterion 2, and SHALL accept each field being left empty.
3. THE Residence_Model SHALL permit the attaching, editing, and removing of an Observation to move `start.latest` earlier and `end.earliest` later only, and SHALL never modify `start.earliest` or `end.latest` as a consequence of any Observation change, those two outer bounds remaining the user's own conclusion.
4. WHEN one or more Observations are attached to a Residence_Fact by any route, whether singly, as a multi-selection, or through the bulk paste of Requirement 9, THE Residence_Editor SHALL set `start.latest` to the lowest `observed_from` among that Residence_Fact's Observations when the existing `start.latest` is absent or later, and SHALL set `end.earliest` to the highest `observed_to` among them when the existing `end.earliest` is absent or earlier.
5. WHEN the user attaches a single Source to an existing Residence_Fact, THE Residence_Editor SHALL create one Observation referencing that Source without requiring the person or the place to be selected again, SHALL prefill its span as defined in Requirement 4 criteria 7 to 9, and SHALL allow the user to edit `observed_from`, `observed_to`, and `page_note` before the attachment is committed.
6. WHEN the user selects two or more Sources already present in the Project and attaches them to one Residence_Fact in a single action, THE Residence_Editor SHALL create one Observation per selected Source, each holding its own independently prefilled and independently editable span, this action being distinct from Requirement 9, which parses pasted reference text.
7. WHEN the user removes an Observation from a Residence_Fact, THE Residence_Editor SHALL leave every remaining Observation's `source_ref`, `observed_from`, `observed_to`, and `page_note` unchanged, and SHALL recompute `start.latest` and `end.earliest` from the remaining Observations only where the value being replaced was itself set by criterion 4, leaving a bound the user entered by hand unchanged.
8. WHERE the value of `start.latest` or `end.earliest` was entered by the user by hand and the Observations of the Residence_Fact support no such value after a removal, THE Residence_Editor SHALL keep the hand-entered value and SHALL report the difference through the coverage suggestions of Requirement 5 rather than by altering the bound.
9. IF an Observation attached to a Residence_Fact has an `observed_from` earlier than the stored `start.earliest`, or an `observed_to` later than the stored `end.latest`, THEN THE Residence_Editor SHALL attach the Observation, SHALL leave `start.earliest` and `end.latest` unchanged, and SHALL display the warning "Källan styrker närvaro utanför den angivna perioden – utöka perioden om du vill." as a warning-level finding and not as an error, distinct from the hard error of Requirement 2 criterion 10.
10. WHEN the user invokes the action "Använd som exakt början" for an Observation of a Residence_Fact, THE Residence_Editor SHALL set both `start.earliest` and `start.latest` of that Residence_Fact to that Observation's `observed_from`, and WHEN the user invokes the action "Använd som exakt slut", THE Residence_Editor SHALL set both `end.earliest` and `end.latest` to that Observation's `observed_to`.
11. THE Residence_Editor SHALL offer the actions of criterion 10 only on explicit user invocation and SHALL invoke neither of them automatically, those actions being the only route by which an Observation value reaches an outer bound.
12. THE Residence_Editor SHALL list the Observations of a Residence_Fact ordered by `observed_from` ascending and then by `observed_to` ascending, showing for each row the source title, the Observation span rendered as defined in Requirement 11 criterion 9, and the `page_note`, and SHALL display at least 15 such rows without truncating a row's source title or span.
13. WHERE a Residence_Fact holds 10 or more Observations whose coverage union contains every year between its lowest `observed_from` and its highest `observed_to`, THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL report zero coverage gaps for that Residence_Fact, consistent with Requirement 5 criterion 3.

### Requirement 17: Merging Two Residence Facts

**User Story:** As a genealogist, when a new source proves that two separately recorded periods at the same place were really one continuous residence, I want to combine them without rebuilding the fact by hand.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHERE two selected Residence_Facts have equal `person_id` values and equal `place_id` values, THE Residence_Editor SHALL offer the action "Slå samman boenden" and SHALL produce exactly one Residence_Fact from them.
2. WHEN THE Residence_Editor merges two Residence_Facts, THE Residence_Editor SHALL take the `start` Endpoint of the Residence_Fact that orders first under Requirement 8 criterion 7 and the `end` Endpoint of the other, and SHALL carry over each of those two Endpoints' `earliest`, `latest`, `precision`, `event_id`, and `note` values unchanged.
3. WHEN THE Residence_Editor merges two Residence_Facts, THE Residence_Editor SHALL give the merged Residence_Fact the union of both Residence_Facts' Observations, each keeping its `source_ref`, `observed_from`, `observed_to`, and `page_note` unchanged, ordered by `observed_from` ascending, with every Observation of both originals appearing exactly once and none discarded.
4. WHEN THE Residence_Editor merges two Residence_Facts, THE Residence_Editor SHALL set the merged Residence_Fact's `role_in_household` to the value of the Residence_Fact that orders first under Requirement 8 criterion 7, and WHERE the other Residence_Fact's `role_in_household` is non-empty and differs from it, THE Residence_Editor SHALL append to the merged Residence_Fact's `notes` the text "Tidigare roll i hushållet vid sammanslagning: " followed by that value.
5. WHEN THE Residence_Editor merges two Residence_Facts whose `notes` are both non-empty, THE Residence_Editor SHALL retain the text of both in the merged Residence_Fact's `notes`.
6. WHEN THE Residence_Editor merges two Residence_Facts, THE Residence_Editor SHALL give the merged Residence_Fact a new `id` unique among all identifiers in the Project and SHALL remove both source Residence_Facts from the Project `residences` collection.
7. IF the two selected Residence_Facts have different `place_id` values, THEN THE Residence_Editor SHALL refuse the merge, SHALL leave both Residence_Facts and the Project unchanged, and SHALL display the message "Endast boenden på samma plats kan slås samman."
8. IF the two selected Residence_Facts have different `person_id` values, THEN THE Residence_Editor SHALL refuse the merge, SHALL leave both Residence_Facts and the Project unchanged, and SHALL display the message "Endast boenden för samma person kan slås samman."
9. WHERE the Possible_Spans of the two selected Residence_Facts are separated by one or more whole years, THE Residence_Editor SHALL perform the merge and THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL report those years as a coverage gap of the merged Residence_Fact as defined in Requirement 5 criterion 2, so that merging 1835–1846 with 1853–1857 yields one Residence_Fact spanning 1835–1857 whose reported coverage gap is 1847–1852.
10. WHERE the separation between the two selected Residence_Facts spans more than 10 whole years, THE Residence_Editor SHALL display the warning "Perioderna ligger långt ifrån varandra – kontrollera att det är samma boende." before performing the merge and SHALL perform the merge when the user confirms it.
11. WHEN THE Residence_Editor merges two Residence_Facts, THE Residence_Editor SHALL derive the merged Residence_Fact's `start.latest` and `end.earliest` as defined in Requirement 16 criterion 4 and SHALL leave `start.earliest` and `end.latest` as carried over by criterion 2, recomputing neither outer bound from the Observations.
12. FOR ALL pairs of Residence_Facts having equal `person_id` and equal `place_id` whose Observations are separated by a coverage gap holding at least one Observation ending before the gap and at least one Observation beginning after it, merging the pair as defined in criteria 1 to 6 and then invoking "Dela boendet här" at that gap as defined in Requirement 5 criteria 11 to 13 SHALL produce two Residence_Facts whose `person_id`, `place_id`, and Observation sets equal those of the two original Residence_Facts, excluding from that equality the `id` of each Residence_Fact, the Endpoint bounds set by the split, `role_in_household`, and `notes`.
13. WHEN an Observation is attached to a Residence_Fact and that Observation's span covers every year separating the Residence_Fact's Possible_Span from the Possible_Span of another Residence_Fact of the same person at the same place, THE Residence_Editor SHALL offer the action "Slå samman boenden" for that pair, and WHEN the user declines the offer, THE Residence_Editor SHALL leave both Residence_Facts unchanged apart from the newly attached Observation.
14. WHERE a Flytt_Event of the same person has a date falling inside the years separating the Possible_Spans of two Residence_Facts at the same place, or WHERE a Residence_Fact of the same person at a different place has a Possible_Span overlapping those separating years, THE Residence_Editor SHALL offer no merge for that pair as defined in criterion 13 and THE Coverage_Analyzer SHALL report those separating years as no gap, the separation being documented as a genuine absence rather than as missing evidence.
15. WHERE the user selects a pair of Residence_Facts for which criterion 14 suppresses the offer, THE Residence_Editor SHALL still perform the merge on explicit invocation, SHALL display the warning "En flytt eller ett annat boende förklarar mellanrummet – kontrollera att perioderna hör ihop." before merging, and SHALL perform the merge when the user confirms it.

### Requirement 18: The Flytt Event

**User Story:** As a genealogist, I want to record a move as one event carrying where the person moved from and where they moved to, so that it fixes the end of one boende and the start of the next in a single stroke and keeps a move-out-and-back tidy.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Event model SHALL define the individual event type `flytt` with the Swedish display label "Flytt", registered alongside the existing individual event types.
2. THE Event model SHALL define on Event an optional `from_place` field holding a `PlaceRef`, following the pattern of the existing type-specific optional field `cause_of_death`, and THE Flytt_Event SHALL use the existing `place` field as the destination of the move and `from_place` as the origin.
3. THE Flytt_Event SHALL accept `from_place` being absent and SHALL accept `place` being absent, an absent value meaning that the origin or the destination is unknown, and THE Event validator SHALL return zero errors for a Flytt_Event whose `from_place` is absent, whose `place` is absent, or whose `from_place` and `place` are both absent.
4. THE Flytt_Event SHALL carry source references on `from_place` and on `place` independently through each `PlaceRef`'s own `source_refs`, so that an origin evidenced by an utflyttningslängd and a destination evidenced by an inflyttningslängd are cited separately.
5. THE Source_Aspects module SHALL define for the event type `flytt` an aspect list containing "date", "from_place", and "to_place", with the Swedish labels "Datum", "Från", and "Till".
6. THE Residence_Editor and THE Event editor SHALL present the two places of a Flytt_Event as separate selectors labelled "Från" and "Till", each offering an empty choice meaning unknown.
7. IF a Flytt_Event has `from_place` and `place` both present and holding the same `place_id`, THEN THE Event validator SHALL return a warning-level finding with the message "Flytten har samma plats som både från och till – kontrollera uppgifterna." and SHALL return zero errors.
8. THE Residence_Model SHALL accept the same Flytt_Event being referenced by the `event_id` of the end Endpoint of one Residence_Fact and by the `event_id` of the start Endpoint of another Residence_Fact, and THE Residence_Validator SHALL return zero errors for that pair of references.
9. WHEN a Flytt_Event carrying a date is linked to the end Endpoint of one Residence_Fact and to the start Endpoint of another, THE Residence_Editor SHALL set `earliest` and `latest` of both Endpoints to that Event date as defined in Requirement 3 criterion 3, so that both Endpoints become exact dates.
10. WHERE a Flytt_Event is linked to the end Endpoint of one Residence_Fact and to the start Endpoint of another, IF the `place_id` of the Residence_Fact whose end Endpoint is linked differs from a present `from_place`, or the `place_id` of the Residence_Fact whose start Endpoint is linked differs from a present `place`, THEN THE Residence_Validator SHALL return a warning-level finding with the message "Flyttens platser stämmer inte med de kopplade boendena." and SHALL return zero errors.
11. THE Event model SHALL accept a Flytt_Event whose date is absent, and WHERE such a Flytt_Event is linked to an Endpoint, THE Residence_Editor SHALL keep that Endpoint's `earliest` and `latest` unchanged as defined in Requirement 3 criterion 4.
12. WHEN the user selects two Residence_Facts of the same person at different places whose periods meet or are separated by at most one whole year, THE Residence_Editor SHALL offer the action "Skapa flytt mellan boendena", SHALL prefill the new Flytt_Event's date from the boundary between the two periods, SHALL prefill `from_place` from the earlier Residence_Fact's `place_id` and `place` from the later Residence_Fact's `place_id`, and SHALL link the new Flytt_Event to the earlier Residence_Fact's end Endpoint and to the later Residence_Fact's start Endpoint.
13. THE Residence_Model SHALL accept the same Source being referenced by Observations belonging to two or more different Residence_Facts, each such Observation holding its own independently narrowed `observed_from` and `observed_to`, and THE Residence_Validator SHALL return zero errors and zero warnings for that reuse, so that one husförhörslängd page documenting a person leaving and later returning may evidence several Residence_Facts.
14. THE GEDCOM_Exporter SHALL write a Flytt_Event as an `EVEN` structure carrying a `TYPE` line holding the value "Flytt", a `DATE` line in GEDCOM date form where the Event date is present, and a `PLAC` line holding the destination resolved from `place` where `place` is present.
15. WHERE a Flytt_Event has `from_place` present, THE GEDCOM_Exporter SHALL write the origin as a labelled `NOTE` line under the `EVEN` structure, GEDCOM 5.5.1 permitting only one `PLAC` line per event, and SHALL record that loss of structure in the export log.
16. WHEN an `EVEN` structure carrying a `TYPE` line whose value equals "Flytt", compared without regard to case, is imported, THE GEDCOM_Importer SHALL create one Event of type `flytt` holding the date converted to ISO form and holding `place` resolved from the `PLAC` value, and SHALL leave `from_place` absent.
17. WHEN a person is deleted from the Project, THE Delete_Service SHALL treat a Flytt_Event as it treats every other Event of that person, and WHEN a place referenced by a Flytt_Event `from_place` is deleted, THE Delete_Service SHALL report that Flytt_Event as a blocking dependency as it does for a place referenced by `place`.

## Settled Decisions

1. **A coverage gap does not split a fact automatically.** One Residence_Fact keeps every Observation and the Coverage_Analyzer flags the hole. An explicit user-invoked action "Dela boendet här" performs the split when the user wants it. Covered by Requirement 5, criteria 4, 10 to 13, and 16.
2. **`Endpoint.event_id` exists from day one, with UI in this spec.** The field is stored and validated, and the editor lets the user link an Endpoint to one of the person's Events. Covered by Requirement 2, criterion 12, and Requirement 3.
3. **Overlaps are allowed, warned only on certain cores.** Two Residence_Facts may overlap freely in their Possible_Spans; a warning appears only when the Certain_Cores overlap, where two cores sharing exactly one boundary value count as touching rather than overlapping. Covered by Requirement 6.
4. **`role_in_household` is plain free text.** No preset list and no fixed vocabulary. Covered by Requirement 10.
5. **Source-neutral entity named "Boende".** Internal key `residence`, Swedish display "Boende" / "Boenden". Covers Husförhörslängd, Församlingsbok, Mantalslängd, and Folkräkning, where a single-year source is an Observation with equal `observed_from` and `observed_to`. Affects the whole document.
6. **Swedish wording for open intervals.** "senast 1840–tidigast 1846", used identically in lists, editor, ansedel, källrapport, and map labels. Covered by Requirement 11.
7. **The period is the user's own conclusion.** The workflow runs period first: the period is recorded by hand, then sources are attached under it one at a time. Observations may only tighten the documented core, moving `start.latest` earlier and `end.earliest` later, and may never modify the outer bounds `start.earliest` and `end.latest`. Evidence attesting presence outside the recorded period is attached and warned about, never auto-widened. The only route from an Observation to an outer bound is the explicit "Använd som exakt början" and "Använd som exakt slut" actions. Covered by Requirement 16, criteria 3, 4, and 9 to 11.
8. **An Observation's span is the person's attested presence, not the volume's coverage.** A volume covering 1847–1853 for a person who moved in during 1851 yields an Observation spanning 1851–1853. The volume's coverage stays on the Source as its `structured_reference` `years` value and is used only to prefill an editable suggestion. The earlier warning about an Observation span differing from its Source `years` value was removed as incorrect, since a narrower span is the expected state rather than an anomaly. Covered by Requirement 4, criteria 1, 7, 8, and 10.
9. **Merging is the inverse of splitting.** Two Boenden at the same place for the same person can be merged into one, with the union of their Observations and the outer Endpoints of the pair, and the resulting hole reported as a coverage gap. Merging then splitting at that gap returns the original pair. Conflicting `role_in_household` values are preserved by keeping the earlier one and appending the other to `notes`. Covered by Requirement 17.
10. **A move is one event carrying two places.** The new individual event type `flytt` holds the destination in the existing `place` field and the origin in a new optional `from_place` field, either of which may be absent to mean unknown, each citable independently through its own `PlaceRef`. One Flytt_Event links to the end Endpoint of one Boende and the start Endpoint of another, making both exact. A Flytt_Event explaining the separation between two Boenden at the same place suppresses the merge offer and the gap report, so a move out and back reports no false suggestions. GEDCOM output is a custom `EVEN` with `TYPE Flytt`, the origin carried as a labelled note since 5.5.1 permits one `PLAC` per event. Covered by Requirement 18, and by Requirement 3 criterion 9 and Requirement 17 criteria 14 and 15.
